# Environmental & Health Advisory Disclaimer

ZeroBon provides physics-informed air quality estimates, diurnal Planetary Boundary Layer (PBLH) dilution modeling, and household routine guidance for informational and educational purposes only.

- **Not Medical Advice**: ZeroBon is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnoses or treatment. Individuals with asthma, cardiovascular conditions, or respiratory sensitivities should consult a qualified healthcare professional and follow local public health advisories.
- **Sensor & Model Limitations**: Indoor PM2.5 projections use a single-zone well-mixed mass-balance approximation calibrated by user-selected building parameters. Outdoor readings rely on third-party Open-Meteo / Copernicus atmospheric feeds with deterministic regional baseline fallbacks when offline.
